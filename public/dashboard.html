              /oauth/token

              – Token revocation (RFC 7009)

              /oauth/introspect
              – Token introspection (RFC 7662)
            

          

          

            GET
            

              /.well-known/oauth-authorization-server
              – Discovery document
            

          

          

            GET
            

              /.well-known/jwks.json
